               ALFA LEAN FOUNDATIONS<https://knet.csod.com/ui/lms-learning-details/app/curriculum/e548a1f4-1136-4d6a-9f5b-e201497dc688>
               The ALFA Lean Foundations is the first level of the ALFA Lean Program, consisting of e-Learning modules. The modules provide the learner an overview of the basic Lean Six Sigma concepts, methods, and tools. This will allow the learner to recognize non-value added activities in their workplace, and identify the wastes (errors or defects) in a process. Recommended to L2/L3+ Amazonians.
               
               ALFA LEAN KAIZEN PROCESS CHAMPION (LEAN YELLOW BELT)<https://knet.csod.com/ui/lms-learning-details/app/curriculum/e548a1f4-1136-4d6a-9f5b-e201497dc688>
               The ALFA Lean Kaizen Process Champion Curriculum consists of 7 e-Learning modules that provide an in-depth look into the Lean and Kaizen methodology. In addition to diving deep on Lean and Six Sigma concepts, the modules illustrate practical applications of the methodology in business settings. The topics include Value Stream Mapping (end-to-end process deep dives), Failure Mode Effect Analysis (for proactive risk identification and mitigation), Kaizen Events (Structured problem solving), prioritization and solution generation tools and Mechanisms. Upon successful completion of a related project, the participants can receive their Lean Kaizen Champion Yellow Belt Certification as well. Recommended to L4+ Amazonians.
